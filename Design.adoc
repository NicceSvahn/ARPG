== Purpose

This document defines the initial architecture for combat, damage calculation,
character attributes, health, abilities, projectiles, status effects, and future
multiplayer authority in the TestGame ARPG project.

The system should eventually support abilities such as:

* Fireball
* Frozen Orb
* Bash
* Throw Knife
* Basic weapon attacks
* Damage-over-time effects
* Area explosions
* Buffs and debuffs

The immediate goal is not to reproduce every Diablo mechanic. The first goal is
to establish a maintainable vertical slice:

[source,text]
----
Player activates an ability
        |
        v
The ability detects or creates a hit
        |
        v
A damage request is created
        |
        v
Damage is calculated
        |
        v
Final damage is applied
        |
        v
Health changes
        |
        v
Hit and death events are emitted
----

== Design principles

=== Use composition for reusable character systems

Shared functionality should live in `UActorComponent` classes attached to
characters rather than being duplicated across player and enemy subclasses.

=== Keep damage as data

Damage is not an object that must permanently exist on a character.

One attempted hit is represented by:

[source,cpp]
----
FDamageRequest
----

The calculated outcome is represented by:

[source,cpp]
----
FDamageResult
----

Neither type is an Actor, component, or UObject.

=== Separate hit detection from damage calculation

A projectile, overlap, or melee trace answers:

[quote]
What was hit?

The calculation system answers:

[quote]
How much damage did that hit cause?

The health system answers:

[quote]
What does that final damage do to the target's health?

=== Keep presentation separate from authority

Particles, sounds, damage numbers, camera shake, and animation reactions should
respond to combat events. They must not decide whether damage occurred.

=== Avoid god classes

No single component should own all of the following:

* Every ability
* Every attribute
* Health
* Projectiles
* Animation
* UI
* Loot
* Enemy AI

Each system should have one primary responsibility.

=== Design for server authority

For future multiplayer, the server should eventually own:

* Ability validation
* Resource deduction
* Cooldowns
* Projectile spawning
* Authoritative hit validation
* Damage calculation
* Health mutation
* Death
* Loot spawning
* Experience attribution

== Core character hierarchy

=== `AGenericCharacter`

Parent class:

[source,cpp]
----
ACharacter
----

Responsibilities:

* Provides the common base for player-controlled and AI-controlled characters
* Creates shared components
* Exposes shared component accessors
* Provides common death and combat hooks
* May later own team or faction identity

Owns:

[source,text]
----
UHealthComponent
UAttributeComponent
UAbilityComponent
----

Should not contain:

* Player input mapping
* Click-to-move implementation
* Enemy AI decision-making
* Individual ability implementations
* Damage formulas
* Loot generation
* UI logic

=== `APlayerCharacter`

Parent:

[source,cpp]
----
AGenericCharacter
----

Responsibilities may include:

* Player-specific animation hooks
* Inventory and equipment integration
* Interaction support
* Player-specific targeting helpers

Input interpretation should usually begin in the Player Controller.

=== `AEnemyCharacter`

Parent:

[source,cpp]
----
AGenericCharacter
----

Responsibilities may include:

* AI Controller assignment
* Enemy archetype data
* Experience reward
* Loot-table references
* Enemy-specific death behaviour
* Aggro or threat integration

== `UHealthComponent`

=== Unreal template

Create it from:

[source,text]
----
Tools
  -> New C++ Class
  -> Actor Component
----

Generated class:

[source,cpp]
----
UHealthComponent
----

=== Responsibility

The component owns current life state.

It answers:

* How much health does the owner have?
* Is the owner dead?
* How much damage was actually subtracted?
* How much healing was applied?
* When did health change?
* When did death occur?

=== Data

[source,cpp]
----
float MaxHealth;
float CurrentHealth;
bool bDead;
----

=== Public operations

[source,cpp]
----
float ApplyDamage(float FinalDamage);
float Heal(float Amount);

bool IsDead() const;
float GetCurrentHealth() const;
float GetMaxHealth() const;
----

=== Events

[source,cpp]
----
OnHealthChanged
OnDeath
----

=== Non-responsibilities

The Health Component must not calculate:

* Armor reduction
* Elemental resistance
* Critical hits
* Weapon scaling
* Attribute scaling
* Status-effect chance
* Friendly-fire rules

It receives an already calculated value:

[source,cpp]
----
const float AppliedDamage =
    HealthComponent->ApplyDamage(Result.FinalDamage);
----

==== Primary attributes

* Strength
* Dexterity
* Intelligence
* Vitality

==== Offensive attributes

* Critical chance
* Critical damage
* Attack speed
* Increased physical damage
* Increased fire damage
* Increased cold damage

==== Defensive attributes

* Armor
* Fire resistance
* Cold resistance
* Maximum health
* Damage reduction

==== Resource attributes

* Current resource
* Maximum resource
* Resource regeneration
* Resource-cost reduction

=== Responsibility boundary

Good:

[source,cpp]
----

float GetCriticalChance() const;
float GetResistance(EDamageElement Element) const;
----

Avoid:

[source,cpp]
----
TargetAttributes->TakeFireballDamageFrom(Source);
----

The component stores and exposes values. The full transaction belongs in the
damage calculator.

=== `FDamageRequest`

Represents an attempted damage event.

[source,cpp]
----
USTRUCT(BlueprintType)
struct TESTGAME_API FDamageRequest
{
    GENERATED_BODY()

    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadWrite)
    float BaseDamage = 0.0f;

    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadWrite)
    EDamageElement Element = EDamageElement::Physical;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadWrite)
    TObjectPtr<AActor> SourceActor = nullptr;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadWrite)
    TObjectPtr<AActor> TargetActor = nullptr;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadWrite)
    TObjectPtr<AActor> DamageCauser = nullptr;

    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadWrite)
    bool bCanCrit = true;

    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadWrite)
    float ArmorPenetration = 0.0f;

    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadWrite)
    float ResistancePenetration = 0.0f;
};
----

Terminology:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Field |Meaning

|`SourceActor`
|The character or system responsible for causing the damage

|`DamageCauser`
|The physical object causing the hit, such as a projectile or trap

|`TargetActor`
|The actor receiving the damage
|===

For Fireball:

[source,text]
----
SourceActor  = PlayerCharacter
DamageCauser = FireballProjectile
TargetActor  = Skeleton
----

=== `FDamageResult`

Represents the calculated outcome.

[source,cpp]
----
USTRUCT(BlueprintType)
struct TESTGAME_API FDamageResult
{
    GENERATED_BODY()

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadOnly)
    float RawDamage = 0.0f;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadOnly)
    float DamageBeforeMitigation = 0.0f;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadOnly)
    float MitigatedDamage = 0.0f;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadOnly)
    float FinalDamage = 0.0f;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadOnly)
    bool bWasCritical = false;

    UPROPERTY(BlueprintReadOnly)
    bool bWasBlocked = false;
};
----

== `UDamageCalculationLibrary`

=== Unreal template

Create it from:

[source,text]
----
Tools
  -> New C++ Class
  -> Blueprint Function Library
----

Generated class:

[source,cpp]
----
UDamageCalculationLibrary
----

=== Responsibility

Provides stateless combat calculations.

It should not own:

* Character state
* Cooldown state
* Cached targets
* Health
* Timers
* World actors

=== Public API

[source,cpp]
----
UFUNCTION(BlueprintCallable, Category = "Combat|Damage")
static FDamageResult CalculateDamage(
    const FDamageRequest& Request,
    const UAttributeComponent* SourceAttributes,
    const UAttributeComponent* TargetAttributes
);
----

=== Initial calculation order

. Validate the request
. Begin with base damage
. Apply source primary-attribute scaling
. Apply source elemental or physical modifiers
. Roll critical hit
. Apply target armor or elemental resistance
. Clamp the result
. Return `FDamageResult`

=== Example calculation

[source,text]
----
Base damage:                         40
Strength contribution:              +8
Damage before critical:              48
Critical multiplier, if critical:   x2
Damage before mitigation:            96
Target physical mitigation:         x0.75
Final damage:                         72
----

Example armor factor:

[source,cpp]
----
const float EffectiveArmor =
    FMath::Max(0.0f, Armor - ArmorPenetration);

const float DamageMultiplier =
    100.0f / (100.0f + EffectiveArmor);
----

This gives diminishing returns and prevents ordinary armor from trivially
reaching complete immunity.

=== Deterministic testing

Random critical-hit rolls should eventually use an injectable random stream:

[source,cpp]
----
FRandomStream& RandomStream
----

This allows deterministic tests:

[source,text]
----
Given critical chance = 25%
And injected roll = 0.10
Then the hit is critical
----

== Ability examples

=== Fireball

Type:

[source,text]
----
Projectile ability
----

Execution:

[source,text]
----
Activate
-> play casting montage
-> animation notify spawns projectile
-> projectile travels
-> projectile hits target or environment
-> explosion gathers targets
-> create one damage request per valid target
-> optionally apply Burning
----

Relevant data:

* Base damage
* Fire scaling
* Projectile speed
* Explosion radius
* Critical-hit permission
* Burning chance
* Burning duration

=== Frozen Orb

Type:

[source,text]
----
Specialized projectile ability
----

Execution:

[source,text]
----
Activate
-> spawn main orb
-> orb travels slowly
-> orb periodically emits shards
-> each shard can hit independently
-> orb performs final burst
-> hits may apply Chilled
----

Frozen Orb requires specialized projectile behaviour because it emits secondary
projectiles over its lifetime.

=== Bash

Type:

[source,text]
----
Melee ability
----

Execution:

[source,text]
----
Activate
-> face target
-> play melee montage
-> impact animation notify fires
-> sphere sweep or capsule trace
-> create physical damage request
-> apply knockback or stun
----

Damage should be applied at the animation's impact frame rather than
immediately when input is received.

=== Throw Knife

Type:

[source,text]
----
Projectile ability
----

Initial Blueprint configuration:

* High projectile speed
* Physical damage
* Knife mesh
* No explosion
* Destroy on first target

Create a specialized C++ projectile only when it gains behaviour such as:

* Piercing
* Ricochet
* Returning to the caster
* Embedding in targets

=== Knowledge boundaries

`HealthComponent` knows about:

* Health values
* Final damage amount
* Healing amount
* Death state

`AttributeComponent` knows about:

* Attribute values
* Resource values
* Accessors and modifiers

`DamageCalculationLibrary` knows about:

* Damage requests
* Damage results
* Attributes
* Calculation formulas

An ability knows about:

* Caster
* Target data
* Cost
* Cooldown
* Animation
* Hit-generation behaviour

A projectile knows about:

* Movement
* Collision
* Caster or source
* Damage configuration
* Hit callback

UI knows about:

* Health-changed events
* Resource-changed events
* Cooldown events
* Combat-result events

Gameplay classes should not directly manipulate UI widgets.

== Multiplayer boundaries

=== Server-authoritative operations

* Ability validation
* Resource deduction
* Cooldown start
* Projectile spawning
* Authoritative hit validation
* Damage calculation
* Health mutation
* Death
* Loot spawning
* Experience attribution

=== Client responsibilities

* Capture input
* Display targeting feedback
* Request ability activation
* Play permitted predicted cosmetics
* Display replicated outcomes

=== Likely replicated state

* Current health
* Death state
* Current resource
* Important status effects
* Authoritative projectiles
* Relevant ability state

=== Conceptual RPC flow

[source,text]
----
Client:
"I request Fireball in this direction."

Server:
"Is the ability available?"
"Does the player have enough resource?"
"Is the target data plausible?"
"Yes: activate and spawn the projectile."

Server:
"The projectile hit this enemy."
"Calculate and apply damage."

Clients:
"Receive replicated health and cosmetic events."
----

The client must never be allowed to authoritatively report:

[source,text]
----
"I dealt 50,000 damage to the boss."
----

== Testing strategy

=== Damage calculation tests

* Zero base damage returns zero
* Negative base damage is rejected or clamped
* Physical armor reduces physical damage
* Fire resistance reduces fire damage
* Fire resistance does not reduce physical damage
* Armor penetration reduces effective armor
* Critical hit applies the configured multiplier
* Non-critical hit does not apply the multiplier
* Final damage never becomes negative
* Missing source attributes are handled safely
* Missing target attributes are handled safely

=== Health tests

* Damage reduces health
* Damage cannot reduce health below zero
* Healing increases health
* Healing cannot exceed maximum health
* A dead character does not die twice
* The death event fires once
* `OnHealthChanged` reports the correct delta
* Negative healing is rejected
* Negative damage is rejected

=== Integration tests

* Projectile overlap damages a valid target
* Projectile ignores its source
* Projectile does not repeatedly damage one target unless intended
* Bash cannot hit behind the character
* Area attack damages each target only once
* Enemy death occurs after lethal damage

    FinalDamage is greater than zero
    FinalDamage is less than 30
    Enemy health equals 100 minus FinalDamage
----

Do not implement Fireball, projectiles, montages, status effects, or inventory
until this path works.

== Architectural summary

[source,text]
----
AGenericCharacter
├── HealthComponent
├── AttributeComponent
└── AbilityComponent

Ability
└── Produces a hit

Hit
└── Produces FDamageRequest

DamageCalculationLibrary
└── Converts request and attributes into FDamageResult

HealthComponent
└── Applies FinalDamage

Events
└── Drive death, animation, sound, VFX, and UI
----

The central rule is:

[quote]
Abilities decide what is attempted. Hit detection decides what was hit.
Attributes influence the calculation. The damage calculator determines the
result. Health applies the final result. Events communicate what happened.
